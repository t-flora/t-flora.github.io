# tflora.github.io

Tiago Flora's personal site: a home for his writing and a calling card for software engineering roles in AI and finance, held together by an interest in learning about learning.

## Language

**Tag**:
A topic label on a Post or Project (e.g. interpretability, C++, education). Readers combine Tags as multi-select chips to filter lists.
_Avoid_: Category, label

**Hub**:
A page that gathers everything one kind of reader should see first. There are two: the AI Hub (aimed at AI researchers) and the Work Hub (aimed at engineering and finance employers). Hubs curate; Tags classify.
_Avoid_: Track, lens, career page

**Pin**:
The one Post currently featured at the top of the AI Hub. Swappable at any time; being pinned says "this is what I'd point you to now", not "this is my definitive view".
_Avoid_: Manifesto, flagship essay

**Status**:
How finished a published Post is: sketch, evolving, or finished. Shown with the dates under the title.
_Avoid_: Draft (reserved for unpublished Posts), maturity

**Draft**:
A Post that exists in the repo but is not published.
_Avoid_: Unpublished, hidden

**Author's note**:
A note added to a published Post, shown above its text, usually saying where the author now disagrees with it. Keeps old views on the record without presenting them as current.
_Avoid_: Retraction, update, disclaimer

**Last reviewed**:
The date the author last reread a Post and stood by it. Distinct from the publish date; updating it is how a Post stays current.
_Avoid_: Updated date, modified date

**Aging note**:
A quiet notice shown automatically on a Post whose Last reviewed date is more than about a year old, saying the author's views may have moved.
_Avoid_: Disclaimer, warning

**Timeline**:
An editable, chronological record of work, education and other experiences shown on the Work Hub. Includes things that never make it onto the resume.
_Avoid_: CV, experience section

**Explainer**:
An interactive essay that teaches a concept by letting the reader explore it (e.g. model-intelligence). Linked out while unfinished, embedded in the site once ready. Not a Project.
_Avoid_: Demo, visualization, app

**Project**:
A piece of built work shown in the Projects section, described briefly and plainly, with emphasis on what made it hard.
_Avoid_: Portfolio item, repo
